Our Mission

Buy it. Protect it.
Sustain the people who call it home.

🌿

Green Network is a nonprofit with a simple but powerful model: we raise donations to purchase threatened tropical rainforest land, place it under permanent conservation, and employ local communities to steward it. When the people who live alongside the forest benefit from protecting it, everyone wins — the ecosystem, the communities, and the climate.

How It Works

A simple model with lasting impact

1 💚

You Donate

Contributions of any size go directly toward land acquisition and local employment funds.

2 🌎

We Buy Land

We identify high-priority parcels under threat and purchase them to take them off the market — permanently.

3 👥

Locals Are Hired

We employ people from nearby communities as rangers and stewards, creating sustainable livelihoods.

4 🌳

Forest Thrives

Protected land recovers and flourishes, sequestering carbon and preserving biodiversity for generations.

Why It Matters

Tropical rainforests cover 6% of the Earth — and hold over half its species

Every acre lost is irreplaceable. Deforestation accounts for roughly 10% of global carbon emissions, displaces indigenous communities, and destroys ecosystems that took millions of years to evolve. The most effective intervention is simple: own the land, employ the people, let the forest live.
